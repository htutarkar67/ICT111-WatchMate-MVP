# Acquisition Metrics — WatchMate (AI Movie Recommendation System)

## Purpose
Defines how the group will measure early user interest, landing page engagement, and prototype validation for **WatchMate**, an AI-powered movie recommendation system that helps users discover personalized movies based on their preferences.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Landing Page Views | Total unique and repeat visits to the WatchMate landing page | Total pageview hits logged | Google Analytics / Vercel Web Analytics | At least 250 total pageviews |
| M-02 | CTA Click Rate | Percentage of visitors who click **"Try AI Recommendation"** or **"Start Watching"** | (CTA Clicks / Landing Page Views) × 100 | Click event tracking on CTA buttons | Target: ≥ 35% click rate |
| M-03 | Demo & Prototype Interactions | Number of users who launch the AI recommendation prototype and complete at least one recommendation session | Count of unique prototype sessions | Figma Analytics / Prototype usage logs | At least 60 demo sessions |
| M-04 | User Feedback Responses | Number of users providing usability feedback and recommendation quality ratings | Total submitted feedback forms | Google Forms / Feedback Modal | At least 20 completed feedback forms |
| M-05 | Early Users & Testers | Percentage of landing page visitors who register or participate in prototype testing | (Registered Testers / Landing Page Views) × 100 | Registration form / Tester spreadsheet | Target: ≥ 18% conversion rate |

# Detailed Metric Analysis & Action Matrix

## Interpretation Strategy: What will the group do if metrics fall short?

| Low Metric | Potential Root Cause | Group Contingency Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M-01: Landing Page Views** | Low awareness or limited promotion channels | Promote the project through university social media groups, Discord, LINE, Instagram, and campus QR code posters to increase traffic. |
| **M-02: CTA Click Rate** | Call-to-action is unclear or not attractive | Redesign CTA buttons with stronger wording such as **"Get AI Movie Recommendations"** or **"Find Your Next Movie"**, and improve button visibility. |
| **M-03: Demo & Prototype Interactions** | Users leave before trying the AI recommendation feature | Simplify the onboarding process, remove unnecessary steps, and display a quick demo or preview of AI-generated recommendations. |
| **M-04: User Feedback Responses** | Feedback process is too long or inconvenient | Reduce the feedback survey to a simple star rating and two short questions, with an optional comment section. |
| **M-05: Early Users & Testers** | Low user confidence in recommendation quality | Showcase sample AI recommendations, highlight personalized suggestions, and explain how the recommendation engine works to build user trust. |
