# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title

**WatchMate: AI Movie Recommendation System**

---

## 2. Target User / Customer

- **Primary user:** University students, young adults, and movie enthusiasts looking for personalized movie recommendations.
- **Secondary user:** Casual viewers who are unsure what to watch and want quick recommendations.
- **Decision-maker or admin role:** System administrator who monitors AI chatbot usage, user chat history, and system performance.

---

## 3. Situation / Context

A university student has free time after classes and wants to watch a movie but spends too much time browsing different streaming platforms and websites. The student cannot decide what to watch because there are too many choices and limited personalized recommendations.

---

## 4. Pain Point

- Users spend too much time searching for movies.
- It is difficult to find movies that match their interests or mood.
- Recommendations from streaming platforms are often too generic.
- Users have to visit multiple websites to view ratings, trailers, and movie information.

---

## 5. Product Introduction

WatchMate is an AI-powered movie recommendation system that helps users quickly discover movies based on their preferences. Users simply chat with the AI assistant, describe what they want to watch, and receive personalized movie recommendations along with detailed information, trailers, ratings, and the option to save movies to their watchlist.

---

## 6. Value Message

**For university students, casual viewers, and movie enthusiasts who experience difficulty choosing what to watch, our WatchMate AI Movie Recommendation System helps them discover personalized movie recommendations quickly by using an AI chatbot, intelligent movie search, detailed movie information, and a personalized watchlist.**

---

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment

*"Have you ever spent more time searching for a movie than actually watching one? WatchMate solves that problem. Simply tell our AI chatbot your favorite genre, mood, actor, or a movie you enjoyed, and it instantly recommends movies that match your preferences. You can also explore trending movies, watch trailers, view ratings, and save your favorites in one place. WatchMate makes movie discovery simple, fast, and personalized."*

---

## 8. Expected User Action

- Visit the WatchMate website.
- Try the AI Movie Recommendation Chatbot.
- Search for movies or TV shows.
- View movie details and trailers.
- Create an account and save movies to the watchlist.
- Provide feedback after using the system.
- Share WatchMate with friends and classmates.

---
