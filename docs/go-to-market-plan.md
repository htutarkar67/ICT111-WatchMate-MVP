# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name
**WatchMate — AI Movie Recommendation System**

## Target Early Users
The primary early users are university students and movie enthusiasts who want personalized movie recommendations based on their preferences, favorite genres, mood, and viewing history. Secondary users include casual viewers who are looking for new movies to watch without spending time browsing multiple streaming platforms.

## Main Value Proposition
An AI-powered movie recommendation platform that helps users quickly discover personalized movie suggestions through natural conversation, reducing the time spent searching for movies while improving the overall viewing experience.

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Class Chat (LINE / Discord)** | University Students | Find your next favorite movie with AI in seconds | Click prototype demo link | Number of link clicks & landing page views | Students actively communicate through class chat groups every day |
| **Instagram / Facebook** | Students & Movie Enthusiasts | Get personalized movie recommendations powered by AI | Visit landing page and try the AI demo | Post engagement, CTA clicks, and demo sessions | Social media is one of the primary channels for discovering entertainment content |
| **Campus QR Code Posters** | Students on Campus | Scan to receive instant AI movie recommendations | Scan QR code and launch prototype | Number of QR scans | Posters placed in libraries, cafeterias, and student activity areas reach many potential users |
| **Email & Student Club Outreach** | Student Clubs & Movie Communities | Help us test our AI Movie Recommendation prototype | Try the prototype and submit feedback | Email click-through rate and feedback responses | Provides direct access to early adopters and student organizations interested in movies and technology |

---

## Launch Sequence

1. Finalize the WatchMate landing page and deploy the live prototype.
2. Share the project announcement with the demo link through LINE, Discord, Facebook, and Instagram.
3. Place QR code posters around campus buildings including the library, cafeteria, and ICT building.
4. Collect user feedback through Google Forms and monitor acquisition metrics documented in `docs/acquisition-metrics.md`.
5. Analyze user feedback, improve recommendation quality, optimize the user interface, and prepare the final demonstration.

---

## Risk and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Low awareness of the prototype | Promote the project through multiple channels including social media, class groups, and QR code posters. |
| Low prototype engagement | Improve the landing page with stronger call-to-action buttons, sample AI recommendations, and an attractive interface. |
| Users are not satisfied with recommendations | Continuously refine recommendation logic using tester feedback and improve movie preference options. |
| Technical issues during demonstrations | Conduct internal testing on desktop and mobile devices before each presentation and verify all prototype links are working correctly. |
| Low feedback response rate | Keep the feedback form short (3–5 questions) and encourage testers to complete it immediately after using the prototype. |
