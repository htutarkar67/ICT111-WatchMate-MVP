## 3. Problem Background

With the growing number of movies available across streaming platforms, users often spend a significant amount of time searching for movies that match their interests. Traditional recommendation methods usually rely on generic categories or popularity rankings and do not fully consider individual preferences such as favorite genres, moods, languages, or viewing history. As a result, users may struggle to discover movies that suit their personal tastes.

Through our customer discovery activities in Lab 03, we identified that users wanted a faster and more personalized way to discover movies without browsing through large catalogs manually. Based on this feedback, our team developed the **AI Movie Recommendation System**, which provides personalized movie suggestions according to user preferences and interests.

## 4. Target Users

### 1. Primary Target Users – Students and Movie Enthusiasts

- **Needs:** Receive personalized movie recommendations based on genres, moods, languages, and viewing preferences.
- **Pain Point:** Spending too much time searching through multiple streaming platforms without finding suitable movies.

### 2. Secondary Target Users – General Movie Viewers

- **Needs:** Search movies efficiently, view detailed movie information, save favorite movies, and discover new content through AI recommendations.
- **Pain Point:** Existing recommendation systems often provide generic suggestions that do not match individual preferences.

### 3. Administrator

- **Needs:** Manage movie information, maintain the recommendation database, and monitor system performance through the administrator dashboard.
- **Pain Point:** Efficiently maintaining an up-to-date movie database while ensuring recommendation quality.
