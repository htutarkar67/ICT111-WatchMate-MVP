# Landing Page Content

## Project Title
**WatchMate — AI Movie Recommendation System**

## Target User
* **Primary:** University students and young adults who want quick and personalized movie recommendations based on their interests.
* **Secondary:** Movie lovers who enjoy exploring new movies and TV shows but have difficulty deciding what to watch.

## Headline
**Find your next favorite movie with AI.**

## Subheadline
Tell WatchMate what you like, and receive personalized movie recommendations in seconds.

## Problem Statement
Many users spend too much time browsing streaming platforms without finding movies that match their interests. Most recommendation systems rely on general popularity instead of understanding individual preferences, making it difficult for users to discover movies they will truly enjoy.

## Solution Description
WatchMate is an AI-powered movie recommendation system that provides personalized movie suggestions based on users' favorite genres, moods, and interests. Instead of scrolling through hundreds of titles, users receives recommendations that better match their preferences, making movie selection faster and more enjoyable.

## Key Features

| Feature | Requirement ID | User Value | Prototype Screen / Module |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AI Movie Recommendation** | FR-03 | Receive personalized movie recommendations based on user preferences. | Home Screen |
| **Preference Selection** | FR-04 | Select favorite genres, moods, and languages to improve recommendations. | Preference Selection Screen |
| **Movie Search** | FR-05 | Search movies by title, genre, or keyword. | Search Screen |
| **Movie Details** | FR-05 | View movie synopsis, ratings, genres, cast, and release year. | Movie Details Screen |
| **Recommendation Results** | FR-03 | Display AI-generated movie recommendations that match user preferences. | Recommendation Results Screen |
| **Watchlist** | FR-04 | Save favorite movies for future viewing. | Watchlist Screen |

## Benefits
* **Personalized Experience:** Receive movie recommendations tailored to your preferences.
* **Save Time:** Quickly discover movies without endless browsing.
* **Easy to Use:** A simple interface makes finding movies fast and convenient.
* **Better Discovery:** Explore movies from different genres that match your interests.

## Call-to-Action
* **Primary CTA:** **Try WatchMate Now**
* **Secondary CTA:** **Get AI Movie Recommendations**

## Responsible Data Message
* We only collect the information needed to improve movie recommendations, such as preferred genres and favorite movies.
* Personal information is kept private and is not shared with third parties.
* Users can update or change their preferences at any time to receive better recommendations.
