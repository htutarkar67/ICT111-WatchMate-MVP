# Demo Outline for Final Pitch

## 1. Opening Problem

- **Too Many Choices:** Students and movie enthusiasts often spend more time deciding what to watch than actually watching a movie because there are thousands of movies and TV shows available across different platforms.
- **Lack of Personalized Recommendations:** Existing movie platforms provide generic recommendations, making it difficult for users to find movies that match their interests, mood, favorite genres, or actors.

---

## 2. Target User

- **Primary Users:** University students, young adults, and movie enthusiasts looking for personalized movie recommendations and an easier way to discover movies.
- **Secondary Users:** Casual viewers who are unsure what to watch and prefer quick, AI-powered recommendations.
- **Administrator:** System administrator who monitors user chatbot history, manages the platform, and reviews system usage through the admin dashboard.

---

## 3. Product Solution

- **WatchMate – AI Movie Recommendation System:** A web-based movie recommendation platform powered by an AI chatbot that provides personalized movie suggestions based on user preferences.

- **Key Features:**
  - AI-powered movie recommendation chatbot
  - Movie title search
  - Trending movies and TV shows
  - Movie details (poster, synopsis, genre, rating, cast, trailer)
  - User login and authentication
  - Personal watchlist
  - Chat history for logged-in users
  - Administrator dashboard for viewing user chat history and system analytics

---

## 4. Demo Flow

1. **Homepage / Landing Page (`index.html`)**
   - Introduce WatchMate, explain the problem, target users, featured movies, and demonstrate the main call-to-action: **Start Chat with AI**.

2. **AI Chatbot (`chat.html`)**
   - Ask the AI chatbot for movie recommendations using preferences such as genre, mood, favorite actor, or previously watched movies.

3. **Movie Recommendations**
   - Show how the AI generates personalized movie recommendations and explains why each movie is suggested.

4. **Movie Details (`movie-detail.html`)**
   - Open a recommended movie and demonstrate the poster, synopsis, genres, ratings, cast information, trailer, and streaming details.

5. **Watchlist & Chat History (`watchlist.html`)**
   - Log in, save a recommended movie to the watchlist, and show how previous AI conversations are stored for future reference.

6. **Administrator Dashboard (`admin.html`)**
   - Demonstrate how administrators can review user AI chat history, monitor chatbot usage, and manage platform activity.

7. **Dashboard & Analytics (`dashboard.html`)**
   - Present Power BI dashboards showing user testing results, AI chatbot usage, recommendation statistics, task completion rates, and usability metrics.

---

## 5. Evidence

- **Customer Discovery Evidence (Lab 03):**
  - Interviews and surveys with university students confirmed that many users struggle to choose movies because there are too many options and existing recommendations are often not personalized.

- **Validation & User Testing (Lab 08 & Lab 13):**
  - Conducted usability testing with **20 users**, achieving approximately **80% task completion**, an average **Ease of Use score of around 3.5/5**, and an average **Interest Level of around 4.0/5**.

- **Product Metrics (Lab 11 & Lab 12):**
  - Recorded **420 landing page views**, a **39.8% CTA click-through rate**, **118 AI chatbot demo attempts**, and **62 active testers**, demonstrating strong interest in the platform.

---

## 6. Closing Value Message

> **"For students, casual viewers, and movie enthusiasts who struggle to decide what to watch, WatchMate provides an AI-powered movie recommendation platform that delivers personalized suggestions, detailed movie information, and an organized watchlist—all in one place."**
