# Slide 2 - Problem Statement

## The Problem

Choosing a movie has become increasingly difficult because there are thousands of movies available across different platforms.

Many users spend more time searching for a movie than actually watching one.

### Common Problems

- Too many movie choices
- Generic recommendations
- Difficult to find movies that match personal preferences
- Users need to visit multiple websites for ratings, trailers, and reviews

---

# Slide 3 - Target Users

## Primary Users

- University students
- Young adults
- Movie enthusiasts

## Secondary Users

- Casual viewers looking for quick movie recommendations

## Administrator

- Manage the system
- View user AI chat history

---

# Slide 4 - Our Solution

## WatchMate

WatchMate is an AI-powered movie recommendation platform that helps users quickly discover movies based on their interests.

Users simply chat with the AI by entering preferences such as:

- Genre
- Mood
- Favorite actor
- Similar movies
- Movie title

The AI instantly recommends suitable movies with detailed information.
