## Notes

The landing page serves as the entry point for the **WatchMate – AI Movie Recommendation System**. It introduces the project, explains its purpose, and highlights the benefits of AI-powered personalized movie recommendations for users.

When users interact with the landing page:

1. **Get AI Recommendations:** Opens the AI recommendation feature, allowing users to enter their movie preferences and receive personalized recommendations generated by the AI system.
2. **Browse Movies:** Directs users to the movie search page where they can search and filter movies by title, genre, language, release year, or rating.
3. **User Account:** Users can register a new account or log in to save their recommendation history and manage their personal watchlist.
4. **Watchlist & History:** Registered users can access their saved watchlist and previous AI recommendation history from the navigation menu.
5. **Admin Dashboard:** Administrators can access the dashboard to manage movie data and monitor overall system activity.
